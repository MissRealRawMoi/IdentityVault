# IdentityVault Token (IVT) – Tokenomics

## Nature of IVT

- Native, algorithmic asset of the IdentityVault network.
- Not built on Ethereum or any Ethereum‑derived standard.
- Can be implemented as an internal ledger or on a non‑Ethereum chain.

## Minting Logic

IVT is issued based on:

- **Verified identity** – only biometric‑verified humans can earn IVT.
- **Useful compute** – devices that opt in to ethical R&D workloads earn IVT.
- **Network contribution** – governance, security, and recovery participation.

## Supply

- Initial allocation for founders and early contributors.
- Ongoing issuance tied to real work (compute + identity events).
- Optional cap or decreasing issuance curve to prevent runaway inflation.

## Utility

- Governance over allowed projects and partnerships.
- Access tiers for advanced features.
- Rewards for users contributing compute and securing the network.

## “Never Ethereum” Clause

IdentityVault Token (IVT) is not an Ethereum token and is not deployed as an ERC‑20 or ERC‑721 asset. Any on‑chain implementation will use a non‑Ethereum environment or remain app‑native.
