# IdentityVault Governance Model (Legal Edition)

## 1. Ownership Structure

IdentityVault is owned exclusively by its founding members:

- **Krystal Marie Nymann — 60% ownership**
- **Peter Chernoff — 30% ownership**
- **Tonya LaFontaine — 10% ownership**

This ownership structure represents legal, financial, and operational control of IdentityVault and all associated intellectual property, infrastructure, and business assets.

## 2. Decision-Making Authority

Strategic and operational decisions are governed by the following authority distribution:

- **Krystal — 75% authority**
- **Peter — 20% authority**
- **Tonya — 5% authority**

Authority percentages determine voting power for major decisions, including but not limited to:

- Platform partnerships  
- Ethical compute workload approvals  
- Tokenomics adjustments  
- Infrastructure changes  
- Legal and compliance policies  
- Product direction and roadmap  

## 3. Separation From IVT Token

IdentityVault Token (IVT) is legally and operationally **separate** from IdentityVault ownership and governance.

IVT:

- does **not** represent equity  
- does **not** grant voting rights  
- does **not** provide dividends  
- does **not** influence governance  
- does **not** modify ownership percentages  

IVT is strictly a **utility and reward token** within the IdentityVault Compute Protocol.

## 4. Governance Scope

Founders govern:

- Identity verification standards  
- Recovery protocol rules  
- Ethical compute workload approval  
- Corporate partnerships  
- Security and privacy policies  
- Infrastructure expansion  
- Compliance and regulatory alignment  

IVT holders have **no governance rights** and cannot influence IdentityVault’s corporate or strategic direction.

## 5. Amendments

Any amendment to this governance model requires:

- A formal proposal  
- A recorded vote  
- Approval by at least **75% authority**  

Krystal’s authority ensures continuity, stability, and protection of IdentityVault’s mission and ethical standards.
