# IdentityVault Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1 – Title
- IdentityVault
- Biometric‑anchored identity recovery for the modern internet.

## Slide 2 – Problem
- People lose access to accounts.
- Emails change, phones are lost, usernames differ.
- Existing recovery flows are weak, frustrating, and insecure.

## Slide 3 – Solution
- Use biometrics + legal identity as the anchor.
- One verified identity → many accounts recovered.
- Works across TikTok, X, Meta, Google, etc.

## Slide 4 – How It Works
- User verifies identity via fingerprint + name.
- IdentityVault issues recovery token.
- Integrations with major platforms restore access.

## Slide 5 – Tech & Security
- No raw biometrics stored.
- Hash-based identity matching.
- Encrypted tokens and audit logs.

## Slide 6 – Market
- Billions of accounts.
- Growing need for secure recovery.
- Platforms want fewer support tickets and fraud.

## Slide 7 – Business Model
- B2B integrations with platforms.
- Per‑recovery fee or subscription.
- Optional premium consumer app.

## Slide 8 – Roadmap
- MVP app + backend.
- Pilot with one or two platforms.
- Scale integrations and compliance.

## Slide 9 – Team
- Founder(s) and roles.
- Advisors (legal, security, identity).

## Slide 10 – Ask
- Funding amount.
- Use of funds.
- Timeline and milestones.
