# IdentityVault Legal Disclaimer

## 1. No Financial, Investment, or Ownership Rights

IdentityVault Token (IVT) is a non‑equity, non‑governance, non‑security digital utility token.  
IVT does not grant:

- ownership rights  
- voting rights  
- equity interests  
- dividends  
- profit‑sharing  
- claims on IdentityVault assets  
- influence over corporate governance  

IVT is not designed or intended to function as an investment product.

## 2. No Ethereum or ERC Standard Usage

IVT is not built on Ethereum, does not utilize ERC‑20, ERC‑721, or any Ethereum‑derived standard, and is not deployed on the Ethereum network.  
Any blockchain or ledger implementation of IVT is strictly non‑Ethereum.

## 3. No Raw Biometric Storage

IdentityVault does not store raw biometric data.  
All biometric templates are:

- processed locally on the user’s device  
- converted into cryptographic hashes  
- transmitted securely  
- stored only in hashed form  

IdentityVault cannot reconstruct biometric data from stored hashes.

## 4. No Guarantee of Token Value

IVT has no guaranteed market value.  
IdentityVault does not:

- promise appreciation  
- guarantee liquidity  
- guarantee exchangeability  
- guarantee future utility beyond the protocol’s defined functions  

IVT is issued solely as a reward for verified human contribution to approved compute workloads.

## 5. Compliance Position

IdentityVault and IVT are designed to comply with global digital asset frameworks by ensuring:

- no expectation of profit  
- no equity‑like rights  
- no governance rights  
- clear functional utility  
- transparent minting rules  
- auditable contribution verification  
- strict separation between token ledger and corporate governance  

## 6. Limitation of Liability

IdentityVault, its founders, and its affiliates are not liable for:

- loss of IVT  
- misuse of IVT  
- unauthorized access caused by user negligence  
- third‑party platform failures  
- compute workload interruptions  
- external market conditions  

Users participate in the IdentityVault Protocol at their own discretion.

## 7. Amendments

IdentityVault reserves the right to update or amend this disclaimer to maintain compliance with evolving regulatory standards.  
Any amendment will be published within the IdentityVault documentation repository.
